1. What kind of projects do I specialize in?

I work across licensed product design, brand identity systems, motion media, commercial photography, UI concepts and AI-enhanced visual production. My focus is always on projects that require both strong aesthetics and strategic communication.

3. Do I work only with one design discipline?

No. My work is intentionally multidisciplinary. Depending on the project, I combine branding, product development, media editing, photography, digital interfaces and AI-supported workflows to achieve the strongest result.

5. International Experience & Collaboration

Based in Hamburg, my work has been developed across European and international contexts, adapting visual communication to different industries, audiences and market expectations.

2. How do I approach a new creative challenge?

Every project starts with identifying the  problem, the audience and the business objective behind it. From there, I build solutions that are not only visually refined, but also practical, scalable and purpose-driven.

4. How does AI support my workflow?

AI is used as an enhancement tool for faster ideation, visual prototyping, advanced image generation and modern content production — always combined with human creative direction and design control.

Each project is built around clarity, modern execution and strategic intention — creating visual results that are not only attractive, but durable, purposeful and commercially strong.
