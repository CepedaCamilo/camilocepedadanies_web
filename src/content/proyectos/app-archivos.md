## What it does

- Drag and drop upload for images and PDFs up to 10 MB.
- A private folder per person, protected by rules in the database.
- Temporary signed links instead of public files.

## What I learned

File storage, upload limits, privacy by default and how to explain security decisions in a simple interface.
